Words of Love

From the Holy Quran & Islamic Teachings

Surah Ar-Rum · 30:21

"And among His signs is this..."

"And among His signs is this, that He created for you mates from among yourselves, that you may dwell in tranquillity with them, and He has put love and mercy between your hearts. Verily in that are signs for those who reflect."

❦
Surah Al-Baqarah · 2:187

"They are your garments..."

"They are your garments and you are their garments." — A beautiful metaphor of how spouses protect, comfort, and cover each other. Each becomes the other's shelter and comfort.

❦
Surah Al-Furqan · 25:74

"Our Lord, grant us..."

"Our Lord, grant us from among our spouses and offspring comfort to our eyes and make us an example for the righteous." A dua for the home we build together.

❦
Prophet Muhammad ﷺ

"The best of you..."

"The best of you are those who are best to their wives." — A timeless reminder to carry love, respect, and kindness into this new chapter of life.
